## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** It showed clearly the list of accounts, its values and the next action.
- **What confused them:** The last touch and value is not clear
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** The details about the sales account and the key risks
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** The dashboard need to be more intuitive with necessary details and show the business value, potential risks and the business impact.
